Privacy Policy

Unpick: Needle Thread Puzzle · Last updated: 27 October 2026

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Stanislav Balakin, an individual developer based in Estonia. Contact: unstitch.game@gmail.com.

What we collect

Anonymous game statistics, sent through PostHog (servers in the European Union): a random device identifier, game events (for example, level started, won or lost), the app language, the platform (Android or iOS) and the install source. As with any internet request, the server technically receives your IP address; we do not use it to identify you. We do not collect your name, email, location or contacts.

Why

To improve the game and balance its levels.

How to turn it off

Open Settings in the game and switch off “Send statistics”. After that, no statistics are sent.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: ads you can watch for a reward (an extra life or extra shelf space) and ads between levels. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may use your device’s advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS), your IP address and technical device information. Google acts as an independent controller of this data; see how Google uses data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent to ads on first launch, through Google’s consent form (UMP), before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time: Settings → “Ad privacy settings”. You can also reset or delete the advertising identifier in your phone’s system settings. If you decline personalised ads, you may still see ads that are not personalised.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases yet. We will add a section to this policy before they appear.

Children

The game is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can ask for access to your data or for its deletion by writing to unstitch.game@gmail.com. Because the statistics are anonymous, we may not be able to link them to you without the random device identifier. Reinstalling the app creates a new identifier.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
